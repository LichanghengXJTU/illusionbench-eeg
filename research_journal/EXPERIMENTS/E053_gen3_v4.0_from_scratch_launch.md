# E053 — Gen 3 v4.0 from-scratch ViT-S/14 backbone retrain (LAUNCHED)

**Hypothesis** (pre-registered before any monitoring):
A ViT-S/14 trained from random init on ImageNet-1K with (a) DINOv2-style
SSL on 2 global views per image, (b) NO vertical flip in augmentation, and
(c) auxiliary 0°/180° orientation classification head will develop
orientation-asymmetric features that frozen DINOv2 lacks. Predicted
downstream: after this backbone replaces frozen DINOv2 in the v3 FTPC
pipeline, ISI > 1.5 (breaking the Gen 1 + 2 ceiling of 1.082) while
maintaining CSI ≥ 1.3 and PWI ≤ 0.5.

**Method (locked in pre-launch)**:
- Architecture: timm `vit_small_patch14_dinov2` at img_size=224 (21.6M params)
- DINO head: 3-layer MLP 384→2048→2048→8192, L2-norm bottleneck + weight-norm last
- Orient head: 384→256→2 (CE on 0°/180° classification)
- Loss: L_dino + 0.1 × L_orient
- Optimizer: AdamW lr=5e-4 cosine, weight_decay=0.04
- Warmup: 2 epochs (10K steps; reduced from DINOv2's 10 epochs at our
  single-GPU scale)
- Teacher: EMA of student, momentum 0.996 → 1.0 cosine
- Teacher temp 0.04, student temp 0.1, centering momentum 0.9
- AMP fp16, grad clip 3.0, batch 256
- Data: ImageNet-1K (1.28M images), NO vflip augmentation; horizontal flip
  + ColorJitter + GaussianBlur + grayscale OK
- Orient view: separate transform (light aug, no random crop, no horiz flip)
  + random vflip with p=0.5 → orient label 0/1

**Pre-registered training checkpoints** (so we can call collapse early):
1. **Step 5000 (~end of warmup)**: L_dino should be < 9.0 (= ln(8192));
   L_orient should be < 0.4 (down from chance 0.693); orient_acc > 0.8.
   If L_dino ≥ 9.0 OR L_orient ≥ 0.6 → COLLAPSE signal, kill + diagnose.
2. **Step 25000 (epoch ~5)**: L_dino should be < 8.0; L_orient < 0.1;
   orient_acc > 0.95.
3. **Step 100000 (epoch ~20)**: L_dino < 7.0; orient_acc effectively 1.0
   (saturated; orient task is easy once backbone has orient features).
